This white paper develops a concept framework for using on-orbit small-particle impact sensors to distinguish deliberate debris releases from accidental fragmentation events in low Earth orbit. It identifies candidate observables such as impact rate, temporal clustering, directionality, size or mass proxies, and impact-signal statistics, and proposes a multi-sensor classification approach for detecting anomalous events consistent with stealth ASAT-style debris releases.
