This project formulates household tidying as a Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP) for a non-humanoid robot operating under uncertainty. It compares Random, Greedy, Basic POMDP, and Enhanced POMDP policies in a grid-based environment with noisy sensing and stochastic pickup success. The Enhanced POMDP uses center-weighted belief initialization, adjacency-based updates, and visit-aware exploration to achieve near-Greedy performance while remaining computationally lightweight.
