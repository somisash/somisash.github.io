This paper examines intentional U.S. private spacecraft interactions with unowned orbital debris and the governance questions that arise as active debris removal moves toward routine operations. Using a hypothetical failure scenario, it maps the current framework across international law, U.S. domestic regulation, industry standards, and company-level self-governance, and identifies prevention and liability gaps for high-risk proximity operations.
