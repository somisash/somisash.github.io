title: "I can’t wait for the summer to fly"
tags: [aviation, personal]
---

I keep catching myself daydreaming about summer flying. I can almost feel the early morning air at the airport, headset on, running through checklists and watching the runway ahead of me.

Right now I am in the slow, invisible part of training: ground school, simulator time, and logistics. But I can’t wait for the season when all of that turns into actual hours in the logbook and the routines of flying start to feel familiar, not hypothetical.
