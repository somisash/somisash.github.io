Tonight I sat down and finally started turning years of scattered work into a website I can actually point people to. Instead of treating the site like a finished product, I built it in public, piece by piece.

I added a talks page with my construction robots presentation from the IET Present Around the World regional finals in France, a teaching section for my peer learning studios at Stanford, and new pages for my memoir, aviation journey, and ski milestones. I also pulled together a working CV and linked it as a PDF so everything lives in one place.

This is version one. It is not perfect, but it exists, and that already feels like a big shift from having my work hidden across slides, documents, and emails.
