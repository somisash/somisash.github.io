title: "Artemis II and old dreams"
tags: [space, Artemis, inspiration]

These past few days, watching the build up around Artemis II and our return to the Moon, have pulled an old dream back to the surface for me. I remember watching Hidden figures and wanting to be an astronaut, long before I knew the math, the training, or the odds.

Seeing the world suddenly look up again has been unexpectedly emotional. For a moment, everyone seemed to agree that space is cool, that it is worth our attention and imagination, and that going farther together is something to root for. The global excitement, the shared livestreams, the little kids in astronaut pajamas — it all feels like a kind of quiet camaraderie stretching across the planet.

I feel proud to be in this industry at a time like this. Even on the tired, ordinary days, I am close to a field that still makes people gasp and point at the sky.

Christina Koch, especially, has been on my mind. Like Katherine Johnson, watching a woman with her experience, calm, and joy step into this mission is deeply inspiring. She carries so many of us with her — the girls who once dreamed of space, the women who are still here doing the work, and the part of me that never quite stopped dreaming.
