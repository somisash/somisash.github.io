I finally did my first blue run today, and I still can’t quite believe it. The first time I tried, I froze and had to call it—but I went back, and back again.

Now I’ve skied it two, three times without falling, and it feels so exhilarating. I can feel my confidence catching up to my courage, one run at a time, and I’m so proud of this feat.
