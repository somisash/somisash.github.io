Today is a big day for us: my son is finishing his ELA CSAPP work, and we’re celebrating in the most on-brand way possible — by going to see the Super Mario movie in IMAX.

This wasn’t just about getting through another assignment. It has been weeks of focus, reminders, small pushes, and quiet cheering from the sidelines. Watching him move through something hard, stay with it, and finally reach the end feels just as satisfying as any milestone in my own work.

I love that our reward is something fun and a little ridiculous: huge screen, big sound, popcorn, Mario and Luigi larger than life. It feels like a way of saying, “Yes, effort matters — and so does joy.”

Part of me is also celebrating the chance to pause and be present with him in this season: to mark that he is growing, that his brain can do hard things, and that we can still be silly together afterwards. I’m proud of him, and I’m grateful that our family story includes moments like this alongside all the everyday struggle.
