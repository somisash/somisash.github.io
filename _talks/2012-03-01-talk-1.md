## Construction Robots: A Lasting Solution to Infrastructural Development  

*IET Present Around The World – Regional Finals, France (2012)*  

Regional‑finals presentation for the IET Present Around The World (PATW) competition, after winning the PATW IET Nigeria finals. I present my concept for using construction robots to automate bungalow construction in the Niger Delta, showing how low‑cost, robotized housing could make basic infrastructure safer, faster, and more affordable in swampy, under‑served regions.
