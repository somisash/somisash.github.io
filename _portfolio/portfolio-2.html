--- title: "Pre-Vacuum Home Robot (Tidying robot)" excerpt: "A home robotics concept designed to tidy clutter before vacuuming can begin.
" collection: portfolio --- This project began from a frustration I experienced daily as a mother of two young children and an engineer: spending significant time picking up toys, clothes, and trash before a robot vacuum could even begin cleaning. I started designing a solution I personally needed — a robot that handles the “pre-vacuum” chores so busy families can reclaim time and reduce the daily burden of household tidying. This concept was grounded in lived observation. I logged more than 500 hours observing how clutter accumulates in busy households, what types of items repeatedly block floor-cleaning workflows, and how people actually tidy before running robotic vacuum cleaners. The sketch shows an early concept for the system: a mobile base combined with an arm, gripping mechanism, onboard storage, and a tray or compartment for collecting household clutter before vacuuming. It captures the initial systems idea behind a household robot built to prepare living spaces for autonomous cleaning. ![Pre-vacuum robot sketch](/images/20260418_212851.jpg)
