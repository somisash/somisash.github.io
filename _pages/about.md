## About Me

I am a grad student in Aeronautics and Astronautics at Stanford University, a mother, and a founder, with a background in Aerospace Engineering from UCLA. My work focuses on space systems, robotics, and the development of technologies that operate in complex environments both on Earth and in orbit. 

I am particularly interested in orbital safety, space debris mitigation, and how engineering systems and governance frameworks evolve alongside rapid growth in the space environment.

---

## Research: Space Debris & ASAT Analysis

My research explores how to distinguish between natural debris environments and intentional spacecraft interactions, including potential anti-satellite (ASAT) activity. I am interested in developing technical frameworks for detecting and classifying orbital events using observable signatures such as impact data, particle behavior, and orbital characteristics. 

This work aims to contribute to closing the attribution gap in space operations by connecting physical observations to meaningful interpretation.

---

## Space Policy

Through my work in a space policy lab, I study how governance frameworks can better respond to the increasing congestion and complexity of low Earth orbit. I am interested in how existing guidelines translate into enforceable standards, how responsibility is defined in practice, and how decision-making evolves as more actors enter the space domain.

A key part of this work focuses on how to incentivize active debris removal (ADR), particularly how policy, economic structures, and technical capabilities can align to support sustainable long-term operations in space.

---

## Space Debris Consciousness

In parallel, I am developing a concept called *Space Debris Consciousness*, which focuses on increasing awareness and responsibility in space operations. This work explores how individuals and organizations perceive their impact in orbit, and how shifts in awareness can influence operational decisions.

The goal is to complement technical and policy solutions with a broader cultural understanding of space as a shared and fragile environment.

---

## Robotics: STAR (Special T Aerospace & Robotics)

I am the founder of STAR, where I am developing specialty robotic systems designed for practical, real-world use. The current focus is a housekeeping robot capable of autonomously picking up, organizing, and managing household items through efficient engineering and integration.

Over time, this work extends toward robotic systems for space applications, including debris mitigation and on-orbit servicing.
