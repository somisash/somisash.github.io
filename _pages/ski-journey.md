Skiing is my main life‑outside‑work obsession, and this page is where I track my progression and trips. I started as an adult and have been working steadily from green runs to more challenging terrain.

## Why skiing matters to me

Skiing gives me a way to push my body, face fear in a controlled way, and experience mountains with my kids. It is one of the few spaces where I let myself play, not just achieve.

## Seasons and milestones

- **2025–2026 season** – Regular trips to Tahoe(Boreal Ski Resort) Take 3, ride free lessons; I moved from greens to confidently skiing blue runs and started to feel at home on the mountain.
