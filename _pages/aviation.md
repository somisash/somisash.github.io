I am training toward my Private Pilot License (PPL) and using this page to document the journey. I started with online ground school, a home simulator setup, and discovery flights, and I plan to complete my checkride around July 2026.

## How I am learning

- **Ground school** – Sporty’s online PPL course and FAA materials.  
- **Simulator practice** – X‑Plane with a Honeycomb yoke/throttle to build basic scan, procedures, and muscle memory.  
- **In‑air training** – Accelerated PPL over 60 days, focused on a sustainable, enjoyable pace.  

## Training philosophy

I want flying to feel like skiing: intensive but fun, with short days I look forward to rather than dread. I prefer a steady weekday pace so I can stay present for my kids and avoid burnout.

## Milestones

- **Spring 2026** – Structured simulator practice and ground school.  
- **Summer 2026** – Flight hours and target window for checkride.
